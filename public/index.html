Introduction

Plastic waste drives global warming, fills landfills, and pollutes oceans.

Pyrolysis offers a way to recycle mixed plastics that other methods struggle to handle.

The process breaks plastics down into smaller molecules, which can be used to make new recycled plastics.

It also produces hydrocarbons that can be used as fuel, turning waste into a source of energy.

The problem

Pyrolysis can be difficult to understand from textbooks alone. I built an interactive 3D application to help students visualise what happens inside the reactor.

3D Model Design

Reactor body

The chamber where plastic is heated and breaks down into hydrocarbons. Modelled in Blender from technical specifications, it is semi-transparent in the simulation so users can see the process inside.

Rotating screw

The screw moves plastic through the reactor. It starts automatically when the process begins and stops when the simulated conversion is complete.
