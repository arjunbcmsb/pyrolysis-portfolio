Privacy

Updated 17 October 2026. This notice explains how this portfolio handles information when you browse the website, use the Unity demonstration or ask the project assistant a question.

Who operates this website

Arjun Chandra operates this portfolio and is responsible for its processing of visitor information. Contact arjun1300@gmail.com about privacy or your information.

Browsing and hosting

Vercel hosts the website, simulation files and chat endpoint. Delivering and securing these services involves processing your IP address, requested URL, timestamps and technical request information. Hosting and security logs follow the provider's retention policies. Static files and the Unity build may be cached by your browser.

The AI project assistant

The assistant uses AI and can make mistakes. When you send a question or select a suggested question, the website sends it to LLM7 to generate an answer. Limited recent conversation is included only when needed for a follow-up. General project notes are also supplied. Greetings and fallback FAQs can be answered without an AI request.

Conversation history is kept in this page's memory and is cleared when the page is reloaded or closed. This website does not keep a database of your chat transcripts. LLM7 and its upstream services process requests under their own terms and privacy policies; their processing and retention are not controlled by this page. Please avoid entering personal or sensitive information.

The portfolio is intended for hiring managers and university-level learners. It is not directed to children. LLM7's minimum-age restrictions apply to its service.

Abuse prevention and usage limits

Vercel applies request limits. A private Upstash Redis counter records token charges, timestamps and a keyed code derived from the visitor's IP address. The counter does not store the raw IP address or question text. People sharing an IP address share a visitor allowance.

Charges count towards a rolling 24-hour limit. Expired charges are removed on the next allowance check, and the counter expires after 24 hours without activity. These necessary security limits are separate from optional visit statistics.

Visit statistics

Vercel Web Analytics provides aggregate statistics about visits, referrers, approximate location, device and browser type. This site does not configure advertising tracking, cross-site visitor identifiers or personal custom events. Page URL query strings and fragments are removed before reporting. Vercel describes its visitor identification as request-derived and discarded after 24 hours; aggregate reporting follows its service retention settings.

You can switch off optional statistics in this browser. This does not disable the website, Unity demonstration or chat. The preference is stored locally only to remember your choice. Browser privacy tools may independently block statistics.

Statistics preferences require JavaScript.

Why information is processed

Necessary hosting, requested project answers and abuse prevention are based on legitimate interests in making this portfolio available and keeping it secure, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Limited aggregate audience measurement serves the interest in understanding how the portfolio is used. No advertising profile or consequential automated decision is made about visitors. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including by switching off optional statistics or contacting the operator.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact the operator using the email above. You may also complain to a data-protection authority in the EU country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. Do not send identity documents unless a proportionate verification method has first been agreed.
